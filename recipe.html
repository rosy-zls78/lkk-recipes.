Quick Recipes with Oyster Umami Chili Crisp

1. Avocado Toast

Spread mashed avocado on toasted sourdough. Drizzle Lee Kum Kee Reduced-Sodium Oyster Umami Chili Crisp on top. Ready to serve.

2. Tossed Noodles

Cook fresh noodles. Drain water. Add one spoon of chili crisp, mix evenly.

3. Pan-fried Steak

Cook steak to your preferred doneness. Rest for 5 minutes. Add a spoonful of chili crisp on top before eating.
